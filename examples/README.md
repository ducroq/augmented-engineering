# Examples — Track 2: Evidence from the Wild

Case studies (Track 1) are deep dives into the author's own projects. Examples (Track 2) are shorter evidence cards drawn from published sources: industry reports, practitioner videos, academic studies, company announcements. They show the four patterns (or their absence) in other people's work.

## Conventions

- **~500 words.** Short enough to scan, long enough to be useful.
- **Pattern-first.** Lead with which pattern this illustrates and why.
- **Numbers over narrative.** One quantified result is worth a paragraph of argument.
- **Source it.** Every example cites where the data comes from. No unsourced claims.
- **Not hero stories.** Examples show patterns, not people. The practitioner is evidence, not the protagonist.
- **Show failures.** What went wrong is more instructive than what went right.
